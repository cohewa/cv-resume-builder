# ATS and role tailoring

## ATS baseline

ATS tools typically extract headings, dates, job titles, employers, skills, education, and keywords before a recruiter reads the document. Parsing is helped by:

- standard headings and reverse chronology;
- one text column and ordinary paragraphs/lists;
- searchable PDF or DOCX;
- direct URLs and plain text contact details;
- exact vacancy terms where they truthfully describe the candidate.

Avoid relying on color, icons, logos, images, charts, tables, columns, headers/footers, or text embedded in graphics. Use a standard font and ordinary punctuation. Test exported text order rather than assuming the visual page is parseable.

## Tailoring procedure

1. Extract the vacancy's title, domain, responsibilities, required skills, preferred tools, seniority signals, and repeated phrases.
2. Rank terms into must-have, useful, and irrelevant/unsupported.
3. Adapt only the title, Summary, Skills order, and the most relevant experience bullets unless a larger rewrite is approved.
4. Mirror the vacancy's vocabulary naturally in achievements and tools; never paste a keyword cloud.
5. Run the quality checklist and compare the final CV against the vacancy once more.

## Product design keyword groups

Use only supported terms, selecting the subset that matches the role:

- Product: end-to-end product design, 0-to-1, MVP, product discovery, product strategy, interaction design, design systems, complex workflows, data-rich systems.
- Research/validation: UX research, customer interviews, usability testing, A/B testing, rapid experimentation, product analytics, hypothesis validation.
- Growth/business: activation, adoption funnel, conversion, retention, monetization, ARPU, marketplace, B2B SaaS, subscription, seller/buyer experience.
- Execution: Figma, Framer, rapid prototyping, AI-assisted prototyping, AI-assisted coding, React/HTML/CSS when evidenced, design QA, engineering handoff.
- Collaboration: product manager partnership, engineering partnership, stakeholder alignment, independent ownership, operating in ambiguity.

## Role variants

- **Master CV:** broad but focused; preserve the strongest cross-domain evidence.
- **Startup/YC CV:** foreground 0-to-1, solo ownership, speed, founder/engineering partnership, MVP validation, and measurable business impact.
- **Enterprise CV:** foreground systems thinking, scale, governance, accessibility, complex workflows, and cross-functional delivery.
- **Freelance CV:** foreground shipped client outcomes, scope, autonomy, communication, and availability; do not imply work authorization that has not been verified.
