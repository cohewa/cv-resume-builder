# CV templates and phrasing patterns

## Canonical current-template structure

Use this structure as the user's current and only approved CV template. Do not generate or recommend alternate visual skins unless the user explicitly requests a redesign.

```text
NAME
Target role · location/availability · email · phone · LinkedIn · portfolio

SUMMARY
3-4 lines: role + experience/scope + outcomes + value proposition.

SKILLS
Product & Research: ...
Tools & Methods: ...
Leadership & Collaboration: ...

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
Company - one-line context
Role | Dates
Mission/ownership line
- Result by action.
- Result by action.
- Leverage/tool by measurable speed, quality, or scope.

PROJECTS (optional)
Project | role/event/date
- Outcome, action, product context.

EDUCATION
Degree - institution | dates

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT (optional)
Verified courses/certifications with links where useful.

LANGUAGES (optional)
Only relevant languages and honest levels.
```

## Summary examples

```text
Product Designer with 6+ years across B2B SaaS, marketplaces, and real estate. Launched 0-to-1 MVPs and improved conversion and monetization through research-led experimentation. Partner with product and engineering to move from ambiguous opportunity to shipped, measurable experience.
```

```text
Senior Product Designer with experience turning ambiguous product questions into validated MVPs, growth experiments, and scalable workflows. Owns discovery through production and uses rapid prototyping and AI-assisted tools to shorten learning cycles.
```

## Company context line

```text
Multi-category marketplace serving consumer and business users across [region], with [approved scale figure].
```

Use the figure only when public and current enough; otherwise describe the sector and product without a number.
